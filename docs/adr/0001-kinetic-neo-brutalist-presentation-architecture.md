# Kinetic Neo-Brutalist Presentation Architecture & Interaction Model

## Context & Decision

For the PISJ-ES Media Society Executive Proposal, we decided against both standard slideshow software and sterile corporate grid layouts. Instead, we architected an elevated Neo-Brutalist presentation system featuring organic card rotations (-2° to +2°), dynamic floating physics, snap-to-straight hover interactions, procedural Web Audio SFX (camera shutter clicks), and structured Bento layouts with operational telemetry artifacts.

## Rationale & Trade-offs

1. **Brand Alignment**: As a creative student media body pitching high-caliber digital storytelling to school leadership, the medium itself serves as immediate proof of creative execution, technical proficiency, and high aesthetic standards.
2. **Readability vs. Dynamism**: Pure grid spreadsheets feel monotonous and fail to command executive attention, while excessive random rotation harms scannability. Organic resting tilts combined with active snap-to-straight hover states achieve tactile visual delight without compromising clarity.
3. **Institutional Rigor**: High-density operational artifacts (SLAs, camera gear badges, academic blackout windows, two-tier approval gates) transform empty presentation whitespace into conclusive evidence of organizational maturity.
