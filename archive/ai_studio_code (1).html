SECTION 02 // VULNERABILITY AUDIT

Failures of the Legacy Model

Past ad-hoc teams exposed the school to 4 critical administrative friction points.

1. Unregulated Phone Use

Unvetted mobile recording during school hours under the pretext of "taking coverage," disrupting classrooms.

2. Lost Footages & Quality

Inconsistent capture quality. Photos stayed on private phones and were permanently lost after students graduated.

3. Admin Coordination Drain

Teachers and Heads had to recruit, brief, and monitor brand-new media volunteers for every isolated event.

4. Compliance Risks

Absence of pre-release approval pipelines created severe vulnerabilities for improper online leaks.

CURRENT REALITY: High operational friction and institutional risk without a centralized charter. IMMEDIATE ACTION REQUIRED
SECTION 03 // ARCHITECTURAL REFORM

Legacy Chaos vs. Centralized Order

How the Media Society directly solves each systemic administrative bottleneck.

DISCONTINUED LEGACY SYSTEM
Ad-Hoc Committees: Disconnected groups assembled and disbanded repeatedly throughout the year.
Uncontrolled Phone Usage: Students roaming hallways under vague claims of school photography.
Local Storage Loss: Raw files hoarded on private laptops; inaccessible to school administration.
Multi-Line Confusion: Event organizers overwhelmed by communication breakdowns.
CENTRAL MEDIA SOCIETY MODEL
Permanent Roster: One trained, synchronized body managing all events exclusively.
Official Media Badging: Zero device usage permitted without physical school credentials.
OneDrive Central Archive: Mandatory direct cloud upload to official school storage.
Direct Executive Line: Single reporting channel to the Deputy Head and Principal.
RESULT: 100% AUDITABLE, SAFE, AND ACCOUNTABLE MEDIA COVERAGE

SECTION 08 // OPERATIONS

The 6-Step Operational Lifecycle

Every single school event follows this unbending, disciplined 6-stage lifecycle.

STAGE 01

Planning

Co-heads assign crew, verify gear, and conduct client brief.

STAGE 02

Coverage

Badged members capture designated media with strict decorum.

STAGE 03

Archiving

Mandatory upload of raw media straight into school OneDrive.

STAGE 04

Editing

Color grading, sound polishing, captions, and graphic sizing.

STAGE 05

Admin Sign-Off

2-tier verification and sign-off by Deputy Head / Principal.

STAGE 06

Release

Final assets published to school boards, screens & socials.

POLICY ENFORCEMENT
Local file retention on private mobile storage is strictly prohibited once the central OneDrive upload completes.
SECTION 09 // ACADEMIC PROTOCOL

Zero-Disruption Academic Policy

Academics are unconditional. Media operations never compromise student grades.

100% Exam Blackouts

  • Zero media operations during mid-terms.
  • Complete shutdown during Mock Exam cycles.
  • Complete shutdown during Cambridge Final Exams.

Class Time Protection

  • Coverage duties must NEVER conflict with core instructional class hours.
  • Rare exceptions require advance written approval from the Deputy Head.

Grade Thresholds

  • Active roster eligibility demands satisfactory academic reports.
  • Any grade decline triggers automatic temporary duty suspension.
"STUDENTS FIRST, CREATORS SECOND" // SAFEGUARDING ACADEMIC EXCELLENCE
