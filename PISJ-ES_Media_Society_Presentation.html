SECTION 03 // ARCHITECTURAL REFORM

Legacy Chaos vs. Centralized Order

How the Media Society directly solves each systemic administrative bottleneck.

DISCONTINUED LEGACY SYSTEM
Ad-Hoc Committees: Disconnected groups assembled and disbanded repeatedly throughout the year.
Uncontrolled Phone Usage: Students roaming hallways under vague claims of school photography.
Local Storage Loss: Raw files hoarded on private laptops; permanently inaccessible to school administration.
Multi-Line Confusion: Event organizers overwhelmed by communication breakdowns and lack of accountability.
CENTRAL MEDIA SOCIETY MODEL
Permanent Roster: One trained, synchronized body managing all campus events exclusively.
Official Media Badging: Zero device usage permitted without physical school credential badges.
OneDrive Central Archive: Mandatory direct cloud upload to official school storage; no local files kept.
Direct Executive Line: Single reporting channel to the Deputy Head and Principal for streamlined sign-off.
RESULT: 100% AUDITABLE, SAFE, AND ACCOUNTABLE MEDIA COVERAGE ZERO LEAKS GUARANTEED

SECTION 09 // ACADEMIC PROTOCOL

Zero-Disruption Academic Policy

Strict institutional safeguards guaranteeing that academics always supersede media duties.

BLACKOUTS

Exam Blackout Windows

All society operations, recordings, and assignments are completely frozen 2 weeks prior to and during Midterms, Mocks, and CIE Examinations.

Mandatory revision isolation enforced.
CLASSROOMS

Zero In-Class Production

Editing, rendering, and media administrative tasks are strictly prohibited during lecture periods. Work is executed strictly during free periods or after school.

100% classroom attendance required.
MERIT

Grade Thresholds & Merit

Members must maintain good academic standing and high attendance. Any academic warning triggers automatic temporary suspension from field coverage.

Privilege conditional upon grades.
CORE RULE: ACADEMIC STANDING PRECEDES ALL EXTRACURRICULAR PRIVILEGES ZERO CLASSROOM SKIPPING
