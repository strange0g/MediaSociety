# Media Society Domain Glossary

Official vocabulary and domain definitions for the Pakistan International School Jeddah – English Section (PISJ-ES) Media Society.

## Language

### Governance & Leadership

**Founding Co-Heads**:
The two primary student executives (Male Head & Female Head) responsible for operational leadership, asset pre-screening, and roster management.
_Avoid_: President, Lead Director, Chief Editor

**Executive Oversight**:
The highest administrative authority consisting of the Principal and Deputy Head who possess ultimate veto and authorization power over all media releases.
_Avoid_: Faculty Advisor, Board, Admin Staff

### Operations & Media Pipeline

**Two-Tier Approval Gate**:
The mandatory sequential vetting process where deliverables undergo Co-Head quality/decorum audit (Gate 1) followed by Principal/Deputy Head executive sign-off (Gate 2) before public release.
_Avoid_: Single-signoff, Informal approval, Peer review

**Official OneDrive Vault**:
The centralized institutional cloud repository where all raw media (within 12 hours) and final deliverables (within 24–48 hours) must be archived.
_Avoid_: Google Drive, Local storage, Personal backup, Flash drive

**Media Society Badge**:
The serialized physical badge worn exclusively during approved event coverage windows that grants camera and access privileges on campus.
_Avoid_: Hall pass, Press pass, Volunteer tag

**Zero-Disruption Blackout**:
The strict operational pause during examination periods (Mid-terms, Finals, Mocks) and normal class instructional periods where media activities are forbidden.
_Avoid_: Quiet hours, Study break, Downtime

**Inter-Society Synergy**:
The collaborative framework wherein partner clubs (MUN, Debates, Sports) retain 100% creative autonomy while Media Society acts as their dedicated technical production partner.
_Avoid_: Media takeover, Club merger, Outsourced team
